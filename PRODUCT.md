# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Akomapa Health serves prospective and current donors, institutional partners,
community collaborators, students, health professionals, and people learning
about the foundation's work. Visitors often arrive on mobile devices and may be
making high-trust decisions about donating, partnering, applying, or sharing
the mission. They need clear, credible information and an obvious safe next
step without needing prior knowledge of the organization.

## Product Purpose

The website communicates Akomapa Health Foundation's mission, programs,
evidence, leadership model, and partnership opportunities. It should help
visitors understand the foundation's impact and confidently support or join the
work. Success means that public information is accurate, financial instructions
are verified, calls to action are unambiguous, and the experience remains
coherent and usable across mobile, tablet, laptop, and large desktop screens.

## Brand Personality

Credible, humane, calm. The site should feel warm and community-rooted while
maintaining the care and institutional rigor expected of a health foundation.
Donor-facing experiences should favor clarity and reassurance over urgency or
spectacle.

## Anti-references

- Gimmicky checkout experiences that obscure whether a payment is manual or automated.
- Placeholder, inferred, or unverified financial details, handles, links, or success states.
- Generic AI-style decoration, random icons, nested card grids, and motion without purpose.
- Edge-to-edge content on tablets and laptops that makes pages feel unfinished or difficult to scan.
- Low-quality partner-logo screenshots, stretched marks, inconsistent logo boxes, or misleading stand-in identities.
- Dense institutional layouts that sacrifice warmth, readability, or a clear next action.

## Design Principles

1. Earn trust with verified content and explicit interaction states.
2. Make the safest next action the clearest action.
3. Preserve Akomapa's established visual language while reducing inconsistency and duplication.
4. Give stories, evidence, and calls to action enough space to remain readable at every viewport.
5. Represent partners and communities accurately; omit a visual identity rather than invent one.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, meaningful
landmarks and labels, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion preferences, useful
alternative text, readable line lengths, and layouts that work without
horizontal scrolling from 375px mobile widths through large desktop screens.
